# The Coaching Business Plan Kit

A one-page plan and a review rhythm for a solo or small coaching practice. Plain markdown. Nothing to install, nothing to buy, no account.

Built by [COACHILLY MAG](https://coachilly.com) as the companion to *Do Business Plans Still Work for Coaches?*

## Why this is shaped the way it is

The research on business plans says two useful things and one uncomfortable one.

Useful: planning shows an effect on **survival**, not on profitability (Hopp, Greene, Honig, Karlsson & Samuelsson, *Management Review Quarterly*, 2018). And planning helps most when it runs alongside real customer work rather than before it (Greene & Hopp, *Strategic Entrepreneurship Journal*, 2017).

Uncomfortable: raising your intention to do something moves your actual behaviour by about half as much (Webb & Sheeran, *Psychological Bulletin*, 2006). Writing the plan is the easy half.

Two things close that gap. If-then plans, where you name the trigger and not just the goal (Sheeran, Listrom & Gollwitzer, *European Review of Social Psychology*, 2025). And tracking progress, which works better when it is physically recorded and better again when it is reported to another person (Harkin et al., *Psychological Bulletin*, 2016).

So this kit is one short plan plus three reviews. The plan is the smaller half.

**On cadence:** no study names a best review interval. Weekly, monthly and quarterly here is a convention we picked because it is keepable, not a finding.

## What is in here

```
plan.md                        the one-page plan
reviews/weekly.md              10 minutes, Friday
reviews/monthly.md             30 minutes, numbers
reviews/quarterly.md           90 minutes, the plan itself
prompts/pre-mortem.md          argue the failure case before you commit
prompts/reference-class.md     forecast from your own history, not your hopes
prompts/quarterly-review.md    run the review as a conversation
SKILL.md                       installs this folder as a Claude skill
```

## How to use it

**By hand.** Copy `plan.md` into whatever you already write in. Fill it. Put the three reviews in your calendar. That is the whole method.

**With an AI assistant.** Paste a prompt from `prompts/` along with your filled plan. The prompts are written to be pasted anywhere: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, whatever you use.

**As a Claude skill.** Drop this folder into `~/.claude/skills/coaching-business-plan/` and Claude Code or the Claude desktop app will pick it up. Then say "run my quarterly review" and it reads your plan and your last review before it asks you anything.

## Ground rules we built in

Keep the plan to one page. A page gets read.

Every priority is an if-then with a trigger you will meet in a normal week. "Grow my referral network" is not a plan. "If I finish an engagement, then I ask for one introduction before the final session ends" is.

Record progress somewhere physical, and tell one person.

Name the one assumption the whole plan rests on, so you can test it.

Write down what would make you stop. The condition, not the feeling.
